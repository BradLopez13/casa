## Full description (4000)

Nido is your home's fridge door, on your phone: everyone's chores on notes, one shared shopping list and nothing to explain to anyone.

WHAT'S YOURS TODAY
Open Nido and the door tells you: "You have 2." Today's chores, the ones left from other days and everyone else's, each with the magnet of whoever takes care of it. Tick one and it goes to the done tray; if you slip, "Undo".

WRITE THE WAY YOU TALK
"Take out the trash every Monday @Marta" creates a chore that repeats every Monday and gives it to Marta. Nido understands "tomorrow", "on Saturday", "every 2 days" and the rooms of your home.

ONE SHOPPING LIST
What's missing, with its quantity in the margin. Whoever goes to the shop ticks what goes in the bag and the others see it at once. "The usual" offers what you often buy, to add with one tap.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
In the shop with no coverage, in the basement, out in the country: what you tick is kept on your phone, shows as "not sent" and goes out on its own as soon as the connection is back. Nothing is ever lost quietly.

SEVERAL HOMES
Yours, your parents', the shared flat: each with its own people and shopping, and you switch from the door.

MADE FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
Large text that is actually readable, buttons you tap without aiming, a cared-for screen reader and reminders at the time you choose. No ads and no data sold.

HOW IT STARTS
Create your home in a minute, invite with a link over WhatsApp and everyone signs in with their own account. The licence is included for now; once billing starts, every home gets a 14-day trial.
