How to delete your account

Nido: chores and shopping

Español · Privacy policy

From the app

  1. Open Nido and go to Settings (the last tab).
  2. Scroll down to Account and tap Delete my account.
  3. If you run a home where other people live, the app first asks you to hand it over to one of them; if you are the only person in the home, it is deleted with your account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out.

If you cannot get into the app

Write from your account's email to nido.app.avisos@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is erased and what stays

  • Erased at once: your account, your email, your name, your reminder preferences and your phone's notification identifier. If you signed in with Apple, we tell Apple to stop associating you with Nido.
  • Stays in the home: the chores and shopping you wrote stay in the home for those who remain in it, without your name next to them. If the home is deleted with your account, it disappears at once and is erased for good after 30 days.
  • Erased on its own: error reports, after a limited time; subscription records, when the store closes them.
