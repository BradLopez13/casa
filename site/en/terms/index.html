You need an account with your email or with Google or Apple. You are responsible for what is done with it: do not share your password. You can delete it whenever you like from the app (how to delete your account). Nido is not meant for children under 14 without their family's permission.

The home and what you write

What you write in a home (chores, shopping, names) is seen by everyone in that home, and stays there if you leave. Whoever runs the home can remove someone or delete the whole home; when deleted, it is erased for everyone after 30 days. What you write is yours: we use it for nothing but showing it to your home.

The licence and payments

Nido may offer a monthly licence per home: a subscription paid by the person who runs the home, through Google Play or the App Store, at the price the store shows before you confirm. Until billing is switched on, the licence is included. Once it is, every new account gets a 14-day trial per home; after that, a home without a licence can still be read, but not written to, until someone pays for it.

The subscription renews every month until you cancel it in your Google Play or Apple account settings; cancelling does not refund the current month. Refunds are handled by the store under its own rules; if you have a problem with a charge, write to us and we will help you claim it. If the price changes, the store will tell you first and you will be able to cancel.

Acceptable use

Do not use Nido to bother other people, to put someone in a home who does not want to be there, or to try to get into homes that are not yours. We may suspend an account that does, telling you first unless we have to act at once.

Leaving and the end of the service

You can leave whenever you like by deleting your account. If Nido were ever to close, we would tell you at least 30 days before, in the app and by email, and you could ask for a copy of what is in your homes.
