Privacy policy

Nido: chores and shopping

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Español

Nido is an app for sharing household chores and a shopping list with the people you live with. This page explains what data it uses, why, and how you can delete it.

Who is responsible

Brad Alejandro López Basantes, who develops Nido. For any question or request about your data, write to nido.app.avisos@gmail.com.

What data we use

  • Your account: your email and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the identifier that service gives you. If you use a password, it is stored hashed and we never see it.
  • Your profile: the name you choose to show and your language.
  • Your household: its name, who lives in it and the invitations you send.
  • What you write: chores, who they are assigned to, when they are due, and the shopping list. The whole household sees them.
  • Reminders: your reminder preferences and your phone's identifier for sending you notifications.
  • Errors and performance: when the app fails, a technical report (version, phone model and where it failed); and, in one session out of five, how long screens take to load. They carry an internal identifier of your account, never your name, your email or the content of your chores.

We show no ads, sell no data and do not use it to build marketing profiles.

Why

To make the app work: signing in, sharing the household, syncing chores and shopping between phones and sending the reminders you turn on. Error reports help us fix bugs. The legal basis is providing the service you ask for by using Nido and, for errors, our interest in the app working well.

Who it is shared with

  • Supabase: hosts the database and the accounts.
  • Google and Apple: only if you choose to sign in with them, to confirm who you are.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and Apple's notification service: deliver the notifications.
  • Sentry: receives the error reports.

These providers process the data on Nido's behalf and only for these purposes. Some may store it outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards the law requires.

How long

For as long as you have an account. Chores and shopping belong to the household and stay there for those who remain in it. A deleted household is out of reach at once, and after 30 days the household, its chores, its shopping and its invitations are erased for good. Error reports are deleted automatically after a limited time.

How to delete your data

In the app, under Settings → Delete my account. Your account and profile are deleted and you leave the household. If you run the household and other people live in it, you first hand it over to one of them. If you were its only member, the household is deleted too and nobody can see it again. If you cannot get into the app, write to us and we will do it for you.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or take away your data, or object to a particular use, by writing to nido.app.avisos@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Children

Nido is not meant for children under 14 without their family's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
